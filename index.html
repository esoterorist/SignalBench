§3Club tuning workflow

  1. Use a measurement mic on a USB interface. Phone mics roll off lows and highs and won't read flat.
  2. Calibrate. Put a 94 dB acoustic calibrator on the mic, then press Set to 94 dB.
  3. Play pink noise into the desk or system. Mic at the main listening spot, ear height, pointing at the stack.
  4. RTA, 1/3 octave, Slow. Store ref before touching the EQ, then switch Reference to Difference to see each move.
  5. Average several spots. Infinite averaging while you walk the mic around the floor gives a fairer room picture.
  6. Ring out monitors. Raise gain slowly; the numbered peaks name the frequency that wants to feed back.
  7. Check polarity with Pulse on one box at a time. On the Scope, the first swing should point the same way for every box.
  8. Watch the limit. Leq with A weighting is what most venue licences quote.
